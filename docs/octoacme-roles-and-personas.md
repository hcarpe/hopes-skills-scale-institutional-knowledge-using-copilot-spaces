# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Release Managers

### Role Summary
QA / Release Managers coordinate quality validation and release readiness so that changes meet acceptance criteria and can be deployed safely.

### Responsibilities
- Define validation plans with Developers and QA contributors
- Confirm acceptance criteria, regression checks, and smoke tests are complete
- Coordinate release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Track defects, release risks, and go/no-go decisions
- Support post-deployment verification and rollback planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to confirm that acceptance criteria are testable and complete
- Work with Developers to plan test coverage, resolve defects, and verify fixes
- Coordinate with Project Managers on milestones, dependencies, risks, and release communications
- Escalate unresolved quality or readiness risks to the Product Manager and Project Manager before release

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Partners help teams identify security, privacy, and regulatory requirements and verify that delivery practices address them before release.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements, designs, and risks for security and compliance implications
- Identify required controls, approvals, threat mitigations, and evidence
- Advise on secure development, data handling, and access management practices
- Participate in security scanning and pre-release risk reviews
- Escalate unresolved security or compliance risks through the appropriate incident or governance path

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to incorporate security and compliance requirements into scope and acceptance criteria
- Advise Developers during design, implementation, and code review for affected changes
- Keep Project Managers informed about risk owners, dependencies, approvals, and schedule impact
- Coordinate with QA / Release Managers to confirm required checks are complete before production deployment

---

## Customer Success / Support Partners

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Support Partners represent customer needs after delivery by connecting feedback, adoption patterns, and support issues to the project team.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, recurring support themes, and adoption barriers
- Help define user-facing acceptance criteria and release communication needs
- Prepare support content, enablement materials, and escalation guidance
- Monitor early customer impact after release and report emerging issues
- Coordinate customer communications for significant changes or incidents

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Product Managers to prioritize feedback and validate customer outcomes
- Work with Developers and QA / Release Managers to reproduce, test, and communicate customer-impacting issues
- Provide Project Managers with stakeholder impacts, readiness dependencies, and post-release signals
- Partner with Data / Analytics Leads to interpret adoption and outcome metrics

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads define how project outcomes will be measured and provide evidence to guide prioritization, release evaluation, and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Translate project goals into measurable success metrics and reporting requirements
- Identify instrumentation, data-quality, and dashboard needs early in planning
- Establish baselines and monitor product, operational, and customer signals
- Analyze outcomes after delivery and communicate findings and recommendations
- Identify limitations or risks in the data used for decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to define success metrics and evaluate customer value
- Work with Developers to plan instrumentation and ensure events or logs support reliable measurement
- Help Project Managers track milestone outcomes, risks, and status signals
- Collaborate with Customer Success / Support Partners to combine quantitative results with customer feedback

---

## Engineering Managers / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers / Technical Leads guide technical direction and delivery execution while helping teams manage architecture, capacity, dependencies, and engineering risks.

### Responsibilities
- Provide technical direction and facilitate architecture and design decisions
- Coordinate engineering capacity, sequencing, and cross-team dependencies
- Review technical risks, trade-offs, and implementation approaches
- Support Developers through coaching, design reviews, and decision records
- Communicate technical readiness, constraints, and delivery forecasts

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to assess feasibility, scope trade-offs, and technical investment
- Partner with Project Managers on estimates, timelines, dependencies, risks, and escalation plans
- Guide Developers on implementation quality, maintainability, and technical decisions
- Coordinate with QA / Release Managers and Security / Compliance Partners on testability, release readiness, and required controls

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Use the interaction guidance to clarify ownership, handoffs, decision rights, and escalation paths during project planning and delivery.
